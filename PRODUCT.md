## Platform

web

## Users

- **Merchandise buyers** at PGA TOUR and TPC venues who select and order Polo Golf and RLX Ralph Lauren for their pro shops.
- **Pro shop teams** who receive, present and sell the collections and need merchandising guidance and approved assets.
- **Ralph Lauren account teams** who send buyers to the site and need it to represent Ralph Lauren faithfully.

They arrive with a task (review a collection, download a line sheet, find their account contact), on a work laptop or on a phone in the shop.

## Product Purpose

A Ralph Lauren destination for the Polo Golf and RLX Ralph Lauren wholesale programme for PGA TOUR and TPC course pro shops: the collections, buyer resources (line sheets, lookbook, visual merchandising guide), ordering and contacts.

## Positioning

Ralph Lauren is the host brand and PGA TOUR is the guest of honour. PGA TOUR appears in the lockup, in thin red accent bars and in course photography, never in the structure, type or voice.
